# APBC2026 representative rosters and ratings

The original rosters were collected on 2026-11-06: Chinese Taipei training squad (18 batters and 15 pitchers), Korea (14 and 14, after postseason and injury withdrawals), and Japan (15 and 13). Taipei changes 林詔恩→張誠恩 and 孔念恩→董子恩 are reflected. Korea uses published club numbers where tournament jersey numbers were unavailable. Australia uses the [official 11 November registration](https://www.japan-baseball.jp/jp/games/apbc2026/player_aus.html), with 12 batters and 16 pitchers.

## Sources and reproducibility

Frozen per-player identities, raw2026 regular-season totals and source URLs: `data/apbc-taipei-2026.json`, `data/apbc-korea-2026.json`, `data/apbc-japan-2026.json`. Taipei domestic players use the same LiveTrend2026-10-06 snapshot as the CPBL clubs. Japan uses NPB official2026 first-team season tables; Korea uses KBO official2026 REGULAR season (series0), not the site's default postseason view. Three Taiwanese NPB prospects use explicitly labeled official farm season data; small first-team records are retained separately rather than merged. Null source fields such as unknown starts are not presented as measured zeroes.

Run `python scripts/build-apbc.py` to generate `dist/apbc-data.js`. It reuses the existing CPBL conversion functions and reference population in `scripts/build-ratings.py` verbatim. Players already in the CPBL playable rosters retain exactly the same ability values. Foreign players use the same priors, shrinkage, percentile reference and absolute power anchors. There is no arbitrary country strength multiplier; ratings compare performance statistics, and do not claim to measure cross-league talent precisely. Running ability is a stolen-base/triples proxy, not tracked sprint speed. Field/arm and unreported velocities/repertoires/movement remain game estimates, labeled in player details. Japanese pitchers have sourced SportsNavi2026 fastball average/max and individual pitch means; the source publishes its own DataStadium tracking tables. Taiwanese pitcher tracking samples retain original provenance and uncertainty. Korean unobserved velocities/repertoires use clearly marked conservative game estimates.

The original nine-position lineup builder selects distinct players with published/known positions where possible; outfield LF/CF/RF assignments and uncertain Korean detailed IF roles are explicitly estimates. Pitcher and batting-order selections remain editable; all reserves, PH/PR and relief pitching use existing game mechanics. Switch hitter 김주원 stands opposite the pitcher's hand.

Tokyo Dome uses official100m LF/RF,122m CF and4m wall height. Between-anchor fence contours and the stadium artwork remain stylized. Source: https://www.tokyo-dome.co.jp/dome/about/


## Australia

`data/apbc-australia-2026.json` records the tournament's names, jersey numbers and handedness. Ratings use the [2025–26 ABL season](https://theabl.com.au/media/), published MiLB 2026 totals, Frontier League and NCAA statistics. The individual source URLs and any older seasons used appear in the player records. ABL stats are available from the MLB Stats API with league 595 and the opening calendar year as its season parameter. For multi-level minor league seasons, counting totals are combined and rate stats recomputed. Recent partial samples shrink towards the CPBL comparison population using the existing APBC formulas. The representative pitching staff includes the verified repertoire for Kieren Hall, Ky Hampton and Cooper Morgan from [Baseball Australia's 2026 WBC game notes](https://assets.baseball.com.au/uploads/2026/03/3.9.26-Australia-vs.-Korea-Game-Notes.pdf). Unverified pitching measurements remain game estimates. Koskei Partington-Hopes had no 2025–26 ABL appearances, so his game ratings are provisional.

Australia's ten additional selection candidates are kept separate from its official APBC roster. The sources include [Australia's 2026 U-23 team](https://baseball.com.au/news/aus-u23worldcup2026/), [Australia's WBC roster](https://www.mlb.com/world-baseball-classic/roster/australia?season=2026), and Australian domestic signings. These are game selection options and have no assigned APBC jersey number.
