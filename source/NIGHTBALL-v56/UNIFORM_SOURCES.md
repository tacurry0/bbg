# Uniform profiles (v228)

Original simplified 3D artwork, based on regular-season looks rather than game
assets or exact replicas of manufacturer graphics. 30 domestic clubs (CPBL 6,
NPB 14 including two farm clubs, KBO 10); international sides also have profiles.
The 2026 season is the target, not announced 2027 replacements. Shirt, pants,
helmet, cap, visor, sleeve piping, wordmark and numbers remain separately editable.
Exact fonts, sponsor patches, anniversary marks, alternate kits and all club- or
player-specific variations are not verified; the table is intentionally a
best-effort visual approximation. No third-party images were added.

match start, both players' snapshots are validated, transmitted, and used only
for graphics (they do not modify local saved kits or match simulation).
